FEATURES

One copilot. Every job.

Alfred sits beside your stream and your desktop at the same time — moderating, clipping, producing, and taking orders by voice.

Your own mod

Alfred watches chat around the clock. Times out trolls, welcomes new followers, and never sleeps through a raid.

Your clipper

Great moments get clipped the second they happen — then posted to Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram.

Your channel manager

One sentence updates your title, category, and chat settings. Hosting an event? Say the word.

Your analyst

After every stream, Alfred breaks down what worked — where chat lit up, where it went quiet, and what to try next.

Your OBS producer

Builds scenes, checks your audio, and takes you live — without alt-tabbing out of your game.

Your whole PC

Not just streaming. Open apps, control music, manage files, run your setup — all by voice.
